# US-301 Pay on one page

As a returning shopper I want to enter delivery and payment on one page so that I check out faster.

## Acceptance criteria
- Given a cart, when I open checkout, then address, delivery and payment are on one page.
- When payment succeeds, then I see the confirmation without a reload.
