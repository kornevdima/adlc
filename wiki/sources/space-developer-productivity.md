# The SPACE of Developer Productivity (Microsoft Research record)

The ACM Queue original returned HTTP 403, so this is the publication record on Microsoft Research. It confirms authors, venue and the abstract's thesis: productivity is more than activity and cannot be captured by one metric or dimension. (confidence: high for the abstract, medium for anything beyond it)

The five dimensions (satisfaction and well-being, performance, activity, communication and collaboration, efficiency and flow) and the three levels (individual, team, system) are consistent across several secondary summaries, not this page. The advice to use at least three dimensions appears only in secondary sources; the paper's own wording was not read (confidence: medium).
