# Team Topologies key concepts

The authors' own site page. It carries no publication date and no individual byline; it credits Matthew Skelton and Manuel Pais as originators and shows a 2021 to 2026 copyright. Confidence is high for definitions because this is the primary, with the missing date noted.

## Team types

- Stream-aligned: aligned to a flow of work, usually a segment of the business domain.
- Enabling: helps stream-aligned teams overcome obstacles and finds missing capabilities.
- Complicated subsystem: owns an area that needs deep mathematical, calculation or technical expertise.
- Platform: provides a compelling internal product that speeds up stream-aligned teams.

## Interaction modes

- Collaboration: two teams work together for a set period to explore something new.
- X-as-a-Service: one team provides, another consumes.
- Facilitation: one team helps and mentors another.

The unit is the team and its boundary, so a handoff in this model is an interface between teams, not between job titles.
