# Shape Up, the Basecamp method

A free online book from 2019, so over 3 years old and potentially stale, though it is the foundational text. Confidence is medium: the chapter pages were seen only through search excerpts, because the direct chapter fetch failed and the fetch budget was spent.

## Phases and who does them

- Shaping: senior people define a bounded solution, look for risks and rabbit holes, and write a pitch. The shaper can be a product person, designer or senior programmer.
- Betting: a short meeting in the cool-down between cycles. In the book's own company the table held the CEO (final word on product), CTO, a senior programmer and a product strategist. There is no standing backlog; unchosen pitches are not stored and must be re-pitched.
- Building: a small team owns the whole project for one uninterrupted cycle. The team is a designer with one or two programmers; QA joins later for integration testing.

## Artifacts and handoffs

| Artifact | Made by | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| Pitch (problem, appetite, solution) | Shaper | Betting table |
| Bet (a project with a fixed time budget) | Betting table | Build team |
| Shipped work | Build team | Customers |

The shaping-to-betting handoff is the pitch. The betting-to-building handoff is the bet plus a circuit breaker: work that is not done when the time is up does not continue automatically; it returns to the table.
