# Atlassian Support - What is an epic?

Atlassian's own help page answers the epic and sprint question directly: one epic is delivered across many sprints, and one sprint can hold stories from several epics. The page is undated, so treat the wording as current as of the 2026 footer, not as a dated release.

## What it says

- An epic captures a large body of work. It is a work type, not a container with its own schedule.
- Stories sit under the epic. The set of stories changes as the team learns.
- A sprint is a fixed time box. An epic is not: its scope may change at any time.
- On a Scrum board the epic groups the backlog; Kanban boards show epics differently.

## What it leaves out

The page gives no rule for how many epics a sprint may carry. The "several epics per sprint" case follows from the two definitions (a story belongs to one epic and one sprint at a time), so it is an inference, not a quoted rule (low confidence).
