# Golden paths (Red Hat)

A vendor explainer, so confidence is medium. The Backstage software templates page (fetched, not filed) never uses the term golden path, so this is the sourced definition. The Spotify original post was not retrieved; the Spotify origin is stated here by Red Hat only.

## What it says

- Definition: "an opinionated, well-documented, and supported way of building and deploying software" inside an organisation.
- Owner: platform engineers create and maintain it, giving teams best practices, defined tools, services and processes.
- Content: software templates (a service skeleton with CI and containers preconfigured), infrastructure provisioning modules, build and deploy pipelines, logging and monitoring, and security policies and scans built into the workflow.
- Home: an internal developer platform, where paths are self-service. A Backstage-based portal offers templates that can be used as is or customised. The page does not equate templates with golden paths.
- Optional: "Golden paths should be an optional way of building and deploying." Flexibility to leave the path is the safeguard against stifling innovation.
- Origin: the template idea is credited to Spotify; Google and Netflix use similar approaches.
