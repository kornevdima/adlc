# DORA 2025 State of AI-assisted Software Development (report page)

The dora.dev report page is a landing page. It states the headline finding and links to a PDF; it does not list the measures, the reliability outcome or any tier definitions. (confidence: high for what the page says, medium for the rest)

What the page itself says: the report finds that "AI's primary role is as an amplifier", and that the largest returns on AI investment come from a focus on the underlying organizational system, not the tools. Google Cloud presents it with research partners.

What came from search summaries only (confidence: medium, secondary): release on 23 September 2025; nearly 5,000 respondents; AI adoption now associated with higher delivery throughput (a reversal of 2024) and with higher instability; the old four tiers replaced by seven archetypes built from several measures including throughput, stability, burnout and friction. Per-archetype shares differ between secondary sources and are not recorded here.
