# SAFe product management

Official SAFe role page, last updated 15 October 2024, no author listed. Part of the page is behind a login, so only the visible text was read. Confidence is medium: the owner and artifact claims are official, but the page fetched gave no detail on the Product Owner relation or on System Architect duties.

## What the page says

- Product Management owns strategy, vision and roadmaps for the Agile Release Train (ART), the group of teams that ships together.
- It prioritizes and manages the ART backlog, the closest match to the "program backlog" in older SAFe wording.
- Its aim is solutions that are desirable, viable, feasible and sustainable.
- System Architect, Release Train Engineer and Business Owners are named as ART leaders who work closely with it.

## Not confirmed

The visible text does not say Product Management owns the feature artifact, and does not mention the Product Owner. Those links stay with secondary summaries.
