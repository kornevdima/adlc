# The Scrum Guide, 2020 edition

The primary text for Scrum. It is dated November 2020, so it is over 3 years old, but it is still the current edition and foundational (confidence high). The fetch came from the official web port.

## Accountabilities

- Product Owner: maximizes the value of the product. Develops the Product Goal, writes and orders Product Backlog items, keeps the backlog transparent. One person, not a committee.
- Developers: create the plan for the Sprint (the Sprint Backlog), apply the Definition of Done, adapt the plan daily toward the Sprint Goal.
- Scrum Master: establishes Scrum, makes the team more effective, removes impediments, serves the Product Owner and the organization.

## Artifacts and commitments

| Artifact | Commitment | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Product Backlog | Product Goal | Product Owner |
| Sprint Backlog | Sprint Goal | Developers |
| Increment | Definition of Done | Developers |

## Handoffs

The guide does not use the word handoff. The interactions are:

- Product Owner to Developers: the Product Owner brings ordered backlog items; Developers select what fits the Sprint with the Product Owner at Sprint Planning.
- Developers to Product Owner: when work differs from the plan, scope is renegotiated without changing the Sprint Goal.
- Scrum Team to stakeholders: the Increment is inspected at Sprint Review, and stakeholder feedback changes the Product Backlog.

The guide has no named design, QA or operations role. Those skills sit inside Developers or outside the team.
