# Facilitation Frameworks — Selection Guide and Activity Designs

## Purpose
Use this reference during Step 1 (Select Framework) and Step 2 (Design Agenda) of ba-workshop-facilitator.
Each section covers: when to use, core activity design, time allocation, materials, and facilitation tips.

---

## Framework 1 — Empathy Mapping (Discovery)

**Use when:** Stakeholders have different mental models of the user or customer. Goal is to build
shared understanding of who the solution serves before defining what it must do.

**Core activity: Empathy Map Canvas**
Four quadrants: Says / Thinks / Does / Feels
Optional outer ring: Pains / Gains

**Time allocation (2-hour session):**
| Activity | Time |
|----------|------|
| Opening and context | 15 min |
| Individual reflection (sticky notes per quadrant) | 15 min |
| Small group sharing (groups of 3) | 20 min |
| Plenary synthesis on shared canvas | 25 min |
| Dot-voting on top insights | 10 min |
| Decisions and actions | 10 min |
| Close | 5 min |

**Materials:** Empathy Map canvas (large format or digital board), sticky notes, dot stickers
**Facilitation tip:** Start with Says and Does (observable) before moving to Thinks and Feels (inferred).
Prompt "what do you see them do?" not "what do they feel?"

## Framework 2 — Impact Mapping (Discovery / Requirements)

**Use when:** There is a defined goal but no shared understanding of how to reach it. Connects
business goal to actors, impacts, and deliverables before writing any requirements.

**Core activity: Impact Map**
Four levels: Goal → Actors → Impacts → Deliverables
The facilitator states the goal; the group works outward.

**Time allocation (2-hour session):**
| Activity | Time |
|----------|------|
| Opening | 10 min |
| Goal statement alignment | 15 min |
| Actor identification (who can help or hinder?) | 15 min |
| Impact brainstorm per actor (how should their behaviour change?) | 25 min |
| Deliverable mapping (what can we build to create that impact?) | 20 min |
| Prioritisation pass (which path is highest value / lowest effort?) | 15 min |
| Close | 10 min |

**Materials:** Large canvas or whiteboard, one colour per level, connecting lines
**Facilitation tip:** The goal must be measurable. If the sponsor cannot state a measurable goal,
spend the first 20 minutes defining it — the rest of the map depends on it.

## Framework 3 — Event Storming (Discovery / Process Mapping)

**Use when:** The domain is complex, involves multiple systems or teams, and the AS-IS process
is poorly understood or undocumented. Produces a domain model and process flow simultaneously.

**Core activity: Domain Event Timeline**
Participants place orange sticky notes for domain events (something that happened, past tense).
Then add: commands (blue), actors (yellow), systems (pink), hotspots/questions (red).

**Time allocation (half-day session: 4 hours):**
| Activity | Time |
|----------|------|
| Opening and format explanation | 20 min |
| Individual domain event generation (chaotic exploration) | 40 min |
| Timeline ordering (no discussion yet) | 20 min |
| Break | 15 min |
| Hotspot identification (conflicts, gaps, questions) | 30 min |
| Command and actor overlay | 40 min |
| Process narrative walkthrough | 30 min |
| Synthesis: key pain points and candidate solution areas | 20 min |
| Close and next steps | 15 min |

**Materials:** Very long paper roll or large Miro board, five sticky note colours, markers
**Facilitation tip:** There are no wrong events. The facilitator's job in the first 40 minutes is
to keep people adding, not discussing. Discussion happens during ordering and hotspot phases.

## Framework 4 — MoSCoW Prioritisation Workshop (Prioritisation)

**Use when:** A backlog or requirements list exists and the group must agree priority before
planning begins. Requires a pre-populated list of items.

**Core activity: MoSCoW Voting and Negotiation**
Items are presented one at a time (or in batches by category). Participants vote individually,
then the facilitator surfaces disagreements for discussion.

**Time allocation (2-hour session):**
| Activity | Time |
|----------|------|
| Opening: explain MoSCoW rules | 15 min |
| Silent individual voting (each person assigns M/S/C/W per item) | 20 min |
| Reveal: items with consensus pass quickly | 15 min |
| Discussion: items with split votes (facilitator-led) | 40 min |
| Must Have capacity check (do agreed Musts fit the delivery window?) | 15 min |
| Won't Have confirmation | 10 min |
| Close | 5 min |

**Materials:** Requirements list (pre-distributed), voting cards or digital board
**Facilitation tip:** The Must Have list almost always needs to be cut. Budget time for the
capacity reality check — it is often the most important activity in the room.
**Critical rule:** Attendees do not own Must Have decisions — the Accountable stakeholder does.
The vote surfaces input; the Decision-Maker or Sponsor resolves ties.

## Framework 5 — Process Mapping Workshop (Process Mapping / AS-IS TO-BE)

**Use when:** A specific business process needs to be documented, challenged, or redesigned.
Requires at least one SME who performs the process in the room.

**Core activity: Swimlane Walk-Through**
Facilitator draws swimlanes (one per role or system). SMEs narrate the process step by step.
The BA captures it in real time. Participants challenge and correct until the room agrees it is accurate.

**Time allocation (2-hour session, single process):**
| Activity | Time |
|----------|------|
| Opening: agree scope (start and end event of the process) | 10 min |
| AS-IS narration and capture (SME-led, BA captures) | 45 min |
| Pain point pass (what hurts? what slows you down? what fails?) | 20 min |
| Break | 10 min |
| TO-BE exploration (what would good look like?) | 25 min |
| Decisions: agreed changes to the process | 15 min |
| Close | 5 min |

**Materials:** Whiteboard or Miro/Mural board with swimlane template; sticky notes for pain points
**Facilitation tip:** Start with "walk me through what happens from the moment X occurs." Do not
start with the system. Follow the human, then identify where systems touch the process.

## Framework 6 — How Might We / Ideation (Design Sprint)

**Use when:** A problem is well-understood and the group needs to generate solution ideas before
committing to a design direction.

**Core activity: HMW Reframing + Dot Voting**
Pain points or challenge statements are reframed as "How Might We..." questions. Participants
ideate solutions per question, then vote on the most promising.

**Time allocation (2-hour session):**
| Activity | Time |
|----------|------|
| Opening | 10 min |
| Challenge statement alignment (agree the problems to solve) | 15 min |
| HMW reframing (individuals, silent) | 10 min |
| HMW clustering and selection (top 3–5 questions) | 15 min |
| Solution ideation per HMW (silent sketching or sticky notes) | 20 min |
| Gallery walk and questions | 15 min |
| Dot voting | 10 min |
| Top ideas discussion and next steps | 15 min |

**Materials:** HMW sticky notes (one per idea), dot stickers, wall space or digital board
**Facilitation tip:** The HMW reframing step is often skipped. Do not skip it. It is the move
from "what is broken" to "what could be better" — that shift unlocks generative thinking.

## Framework 7 — Retrospective (Retrospective)

**Note:** This framework covers BA-project retrospectives, not Scrum Sprint Retrospectives.
For Scrum Sprint Retrospectives, use ba-scrum-events-pack.

**Use when:** A project phase, workstream, or initiative has completed and the team needs to
capture lessons learned and agree improvements.

**Core activity: Four Ls (Liked / Learned / Lacked / Longed For)**
More structured than Start/Stop/Continue for BA project contexts. Produces both reflection and
actionable improvement items.

**Time allocation (90-minute session):**
| Activity | Time |
|----------|------|
| Opening: set the tone (safe space, no blame) | 10 min |
| Individual reflection, all four Ls (silent sticky notes) | 15 min |
| Small group sharing and clustering | 20 min |
| Plenary: top themes per L | 20 min |
| Action generation from Lacked and Longed For | 15 min |
| Action owner assignment and prioritisation | 10 min |

**Materials:** Four-quadrant canvas, sticky notes per quadrant
**Facilitation tip:** "Lacked" and "Longed For" are where improvement actions come from.
Do not let the session end without at least three committed actions with owners and dates.
Liked and Learned are for appreciation and knowledge sharing only.
